### 🚀 Featured Project

**[MacroScript — Macroeconomic Research & Model Validation](https://github.com/syedomarali2001/MacroScript)**

A Python/Streamlit dashboard for exploring the U.S. economy today and reconstructing what economic information was available at historical points in time.

- Integrates **FRED/ALFRED** economic data and **304 monthly point-in-time snapshots**.
- Analyzes unemployment, payrolls, industrial production, housing permits, and the Treasury yield spread.
- Compares historical-vintage data with subsequently revised figures to highlight **data revisions and look-ahead bias**.
- Documents recession-risk research using chronological backtesting, calibration, and robustness analysis; unreliable forecasting models were **not deployed**.
