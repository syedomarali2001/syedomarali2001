#### 📊 [Portfolio Risk Engine — Quantitative Portfolio Analytics & Optimization](https://github.com/syedomarali2001/PortfolioRiskEngine)

An interactive Python/Streamlit application for building investment portfolios, analyzing risk and performance, and exploring alternative allocations.

- Implements **Value at Risk (VaR), Expected Shortfall, Monte Carlo simulation, stress testing, and statistical backtesting**.
- Compares portfolio returns, volatility, Sharpe/Sortino ratios, and drawdowns against **SPY and RSP**.
- Explores three portfolio optimization approaches: **Growth, Balanced, and Stability**.
- Includes ETF exposure analysis, dividend projections, stock valuation, and interactive risk dashboards.
- Uses a modular Python backend with **automated tests and GitHub Actions**.

#### 🌎 [MacroScript — Macroeconomic Research & Model Validation](https://github.com/syedomarali2001/MacroScript)

A Python/Streamlit dashboard for exploring the U.S. economy today and reconstructing what economic information was actually available at historical points in time.

- Integrates **FRED/ALFRED** economic data and **304 monthly point-in-time snapshots** in the local historical archive.
- Analyzes unemployment, payrolls, industrial production, housing permits, and the Treasury yield spread.
- Features an interactive **Time Machine** for examining historical economic conditions before later revisions.
- Compares historical-vintage data with revised figures to investigate **data revisions and look-ahead bias**.
- Documents recession-risk research using chronological backtesting, calibration, and robustness analysis. Unreliable forecasting models were **not deployed**.
